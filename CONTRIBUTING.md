# Contributing

Community-built tools for the [Quilibrium](https://quilibrium.com) network. See everything we host on the [organization page](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community).

## Working on a project here

Fork the repo, make your changes on a branch, open a pull request against `main`. No permission needed, and you do not have to join anything.

We are volunteers, so replies can take a while. If you have heard nothing after a couple of weeks, say so again. It means we lost it, not that we hate it.

To talk an idea through first, reach us through the [community links page](https://quilibrium.one/links).

### Contributing often?

If you keep showing up on a project, we can add you as a collaborator on it, and you should feel free to ask rather than wait to be offered.

It does not change what you are allowed to propose, since anyone can open a pull request from a fork. What it changes is that you push branches straight to the repo instead of keeping a fork in sync, and branch builds get access to the project's CI secrets, which pull requests from forks do not. For anyone contributing regularly that is the difference between pleasant and tedious.

This is per project, not org-wide, and it gives you no visibility into anything else here. If you stay involved across the organization over time, full membership is the step after that.

## Built something of your own?

Tell us about it. We would like to see it, we are happy to help with issues and pull requests, and we will point people at it.

**Keep it under your own account, though.** We rarely take outside projects into the organization, and that is worth being straight about rather than leaving you to guess. Hosting a project here means we own it, and when it breaks or goes quiet it becomes our problem. We are a small group of volunteers, so a shelf of half-maintained repositories with our name on them is the one outcome we most want to avoid.

When it does happen, it grows out of having worked together for a while. It is not really something you apply for, and there is no queue you can join. So the useful move is not to ask, it is to build the thing and be around. If it turns out we are relying on your project six months from now, that conversation will start itself.

Nothing is lost by waiting either way. Moving a repository later is exactly as easy as moving it today.

If you think your project is already central to the community, say so anyway: [open an issue](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/issues/new?title=Project:%20YOUR%20PROJECT&body=What%20it%20does:%0A%0ARepo%20link:%0A%0AHow%20long%20it%20has%20been%20running:%0A). Worst case we say not yet and we have still seen your work.

## Rules for anything hosted here

1. Open source under AGPL-3.0, matching the official Quilibrium projects.
2. Actually about Quilibrium.
3. No impersonating official Quilibrium projects. Everything here is community-run and unofficial, and says so.

## Questions

About a specific project, open an issue on that project's own repository.

About anything else: [open an issue on the organization repository](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/issues), or find us through the [community links page](https://quilibrium.one/links).
