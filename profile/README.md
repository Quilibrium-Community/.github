> This organization is community-driven and is **not affiliated with or endorsed by Quilibrium Inc.**

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from anyone interested in the Quilibrium ecosystem.

1. **Fork** the repo you want to contribute to
2. Make your changes and open a **Pull Request**
3. Join the discussion on [Discord](https://discord.gg/quilibrium) in the `#dev-general` channel

No special permissions needed — just fork and submit a PR.

## Community Treasury

The community runs a treasury to fund ecosystem growth — educational content, developer grants, CEX listings, and community programs.

The treasury is managed via a Gnosis Safe multisig (3-of-5 signatures required) with full transparency reports posted monthly on Discord.

**[Learn more & donate](https://quilibrium.one/#treasury)**
