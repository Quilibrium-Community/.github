Community-built open-source tools and resources for the [Quilibrium](https://quilibrium.com) network — the decentralized internet protocol.

> This organization is community-driven and is **not affiliated with or endorsed by Quilibrium Inc.**

## Project Guidelines

All projects in this organization must:

1. **Be open source** under the **AGPL-3.0** license, aligned with official Quilibrium projects
2. **Be related to Quilibrium** — community tools, resources, bots, integrations, or ecosystem utilities
3. **Not impersonate official Quilibrium projects** — all projects must clearly state they are community-driven and unofficial

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from anyone interested in the Quilibrium ecosystem.

1. **Fork** the repo you want to contribute to
2. Make your changes and open a **Pull Request**
3. Join the discussion on [Discord](https://discord.gg/quilibrium) in the `#dev-general` channel

No special permissions needed — just fork and submit a PR.

**Built something for the Quilibrium ecosystem?** We would like to see it, even though projects usually stay under their author's own account. The [contributing guide](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to get involved and what we can host.

We also keep a list of [community projects](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/blob/main/COMMUNITY-PROJECTS.md) built by people we know, hosted on their own accounts rather than here.

## Community Treasury

The community runs a treasury to fund ecosystem growth — educational content, developer grants, CEX listings, and community programs.

The treasury is managed via a Gnosis Safe multisig (3-of-5 signatures required) with full transparency reports posted monthly on Discord.

**[Learn more & donate](https://quilibrium.one/#treasury)**
