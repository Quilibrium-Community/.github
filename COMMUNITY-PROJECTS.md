# Community projects

Projects built by people in the Quilibrium community. **They are not part of this organization.** Each one is owned and maintained by its author, on their own account.

We link them because we know the people who built them, not because we have reviewed their code. We do not control what they release, we cannot patch them, and we cannot make promises about them. Treat them as you would any other third-party software.

**Anything that touches private keys, seed phrases, or funds deserves extra care.** Nobody here has audited these tools. If something asks for your key, make sure you understand what it does with it first. The table flags which projects those are.

Seen something stale or wrong? [Tell us](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/issues) and we will remove it. We would rather delist fast than leave a bad link up.

## Getting listed

There is no application. We list projects by people we have worked with, because the only thing we are really vouching for is that we know who is behind it.

So the way onto this list is the same as the way into anything else here: build the thing, be around, contribute. If we end up using what you built, we will want to point people at it.

If you think your project belongs here and we have somehow missed it, [say so](https://github.com/Quilibrium-Community/.github/issues).

## Removal

We remove a project from this list, without it being a judgement on anyone, when:

- the author asks us to
- it stops working and nobody is fixing it
- the author becomes unreachable
- something about it worries us

Delisting is cheap and reversible. We would rather take something down early and put it back later than leave a link up that hurts someone.
